Our Journey

One Group.
One Thread.

From a single spinning unit to three integrated manufacturing facilities across India — the story of how Prateek Group was built, year by year.

The Beginning Expansion Integration Today

Built on Legacy.
Driven by Innovation.

Every yarn we spin today carries the weight of years of decisions — the first machine installed, the first export order, the first facility built from the ground up.

What follows is that journey, told through the milestones that shaped Prateek Group into the integrated manufacturing group it is today.

Milestones

The Road So Far

The Beginning

A Single Spinning Unit in Bhiwandi

Prateek Group began with one modest spinning floor in Bhiwandi, Maharashtra — a handful of machines, a small team, and a commitment to getting every count right the first time.

Bhiwandi Spinning

Craft

Moving Beyond Commodity Yarn

Rather than compete on volume alone, the focus shifted to fancy and value-added yarns — 2-ply and 3-ply constructions built for texture, character and finish that standard threads could not offer.

Fancy Yarn 300+ Variants

Expansion

KK Spintex Opens in Guntur

A second facility in Guntur, Andhra Pradesh brought large-scale polyester-viscose production in house, pairing polyester's durability with viscose's silk-like drape across custom blend ratios.

Guntur PV Yarn

Integration

GB Dyed Yarn Completes the Chain

With the dyeing and texturizing unit at Palsana, Gujarat, the group closed the loop — from raw spinning through to finished, colour-fast dyed yarn, all under one roof.

Palsana Dyeing

Scale

38,000 Spindles and Counting

Sustained investment in machinery and quality control pushed combined capacity past 900 metric tonnes a month, backed by in-house labs and a complaint-free record with long-standing customers.

38,000+ Spindles 900+ MT / Month

Today

Three Facilities. One Integrated Group.

Fifteen years on, Prateek Group operates three specialised plants across three states — serving shirting, suiting, dress material and home furnishing manufacturers throughout India.

3 Facilities 15+ Years
